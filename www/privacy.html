SkyRoute Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

SkyRoute is a navigation app for riders that shows weather along your route. It is built to work without an account and without collecting personal information.

Location

SkyRoute uses your device's location to show your position on the map, give turn-by-turn directions, and fetch weather for where you are and where you're going. Your location is sent only to the services that need it to answer a request — the routing server, the weather service, the geocoder, and the U.S. National Weather Service alert service — and only for that request. SkyRoute does not store your location history on any server, and we do not have servers of our own that receive it.

Ride log

When you finish a route, SkyRoute saves a summary (destination, date, distance, duration) on your device only. You can delete any ride from the "My rides" screen. Sharing a ride creates a link containing only the destination coordinates and name — nothing about you.

Third-party services

To work, the app requests data from: OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap (map tiles), Valhalla and OSRM public routers (directions), Photon and Nominatim (place search), Open-Meteo (weather), RainViewer (radar), and api.weather.gov (alerts). These requests include the coordinates needed to answer them and standard technical information such as your IP address, governed by each provider's own policy.

What we do not do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data. The app does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Children

SkyRoute is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
